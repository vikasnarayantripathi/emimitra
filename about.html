Our Mission

Why We Built EMI Mitra

Taking a loan is one of the biggest financial decisions an Indian family makes. Yet most people don't fully understand what they're signing up for — how much total interest they'll pay, whether they can afford the EMI, or if they're getting the best rate.

EMI Mitra exists to change that. We provide clear, accurate, and instant loan calculations so every Indian — whether in Mumbai or Mau, Bangalore or Bhagalpur — can make informed financial decisions.

🎯

Accuracy First

We use the same reducing balance formula used by all RBI-regulated banks. Every calculation is precise to the rupee.

🆓

Always Free

🇮🇳

Built for India

Transparency

How EMI Mitra Makes Money

EMI Mitra is free for all users. We generate revenue through two sources:

1. Display Advertising: We display advertisements via Google AdSense. These ads are served by Google and are based on your browsing behavior and our website content.

2. Affiliate Partnerships: When you click "Apply Now" or "Check Eligibility" buttons on our site, you are redirected to our lending partners like PaisaBazaar and BankBazaar. If you apply for and receive a loan through these partners, we earn a referral commission. This does not affect the loan terms, interest rate or any charges you pay.

Important: EMI Mitra is not a bank, NBFC, or financial advisor. All calculations are for informational purposes only. Always verify loan terms directly with your bank or lender before making financial decisions. Loan approval, interest rates, and terms are subject to the lender's sole discretion.
