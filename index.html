What is EMI &
How is it Calculated?

📘 What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is a fixed monthly payment made to a lender. It includes both principal repayment and interest, spread evenly across the loan tenure so you pay the same amount every month.

🧮 EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ
      ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ – 1)

P = Principal amount
r = Monthly interest rate
n = Total monthly payments

📊 What Affects EMI?

  • → Higher loan amount = Higher EMI
  • → Lower interest rate = Lower EMI
  • → Longer tenure = Lower EMI
  • → Better CIBIL score = Lower rate

What is a healthy EMI to salary ratio?+
Banks recommend keeping total monthly EMIs under 40–50% of gross monthly income. For ₹60,000 salary, total EMIs should not exceed ₹24,000–30,000.
How can I reduce my loan EMI?+
1) Increase loan tenure, 2) Negotiate lower interest rate, 3) Make a larger down payment, 4) Improve your CIBIL score above 750 before applying.
Should prepayment reduce EMI or tenure?+
Reducing tenure saves significantly more on total interest. Choose to reduce tenure if you have no cash flow pressure — you'll save lakhs over the loan period.
