State Bank of India (SBI) is the largest home loan lender in the country, and its home loan rates set the benchmark much of the market follows. If you're planning to buy a home, understanding how your SBI home loan EMI is calculated — and what actually drives it up or down — can save you lakhs over the life of the loan. This guide breaks it all down in plain language.

Current SBI Home Loan Interest Rates (2026)

SBI home loan interest rates are linked to the RBI repo rate and vary based on your CIBIL score, loan amount, and whether you're a salaried or self-employed applicant. The figures below are indicative ranges for 2026 — always confirm the live rate with the bank before applying.

How SBI Calculates Your EMI

SBI, like all RBI-regulated banks, uses the reducing balance method. The formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ [(1+r)n − 1]

Where P is the principal (loan amount), r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of monthly instalments (years × 12). In the early years, most of your EMI goes toward interest; over time, more goes toward the principal.

SBI Home Loan Eligibility

To qualify for an SBI home loan, you generally need to meet these criteria:

  • Age: 18 to 70 years (loan must be repaid before you turn 70)
  • Income: Stable income with EMIs ideally under 50% of net monthly income
  • Credit score: 750+ for the best rates and smooth approval
  • Employment: Salaried, self-employed professionals, or business owners

Documents Required

  • Identity & address proof (Aadhaar, PAN, passport)
  • Income proof (salary slips, Form 16, or ITR for self-employed)
  • Bank statements (last 6 months)
  • Property documents (sale agreement, title deed)

5 Ways to Reduce Your SBI Home Loan EMI

  1. Improve your CIBIL score before applying — even a small rate cut saves lakhs.
  2. Make a larger down payment to reduce the principal and the EMI.
  3. Choose a longer tenure if you need a lower monthly outflow (but you'll pay more total interest).
  4. Make prepayments when you have surplus cash — see how much you'd save with our Prepayment Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the EMI for a ₹30 lakh SBI home loan?
At around 8.5% interest for 20 years, the EMI is approximately ₹26,035 per month. Use the calculator above for your exact rate and tenure.
What is the minimum salary for an SBI home loan?
There's no fixed minimum, but your income should comfortably keep the EMI within about 50% of your net monthly salary. A CIBIL score above 750 improves both eligibility and rate.
Is there a prepayment penalty on SBI home loans?
As per RBI rules, there is no prepayment penalty on floating-rate home loans. You can prepay any amount to reduce your tenure or EMI without extra charges.
Can I transfer my existing home loan to SBI?
Yes. If SBI offers a lower rate than your current lender, a balance transfer can save significant interest. Factor in the processing fee — use our Balance Transfer Calculator to check if it's worth it.
