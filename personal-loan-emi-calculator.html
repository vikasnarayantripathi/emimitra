About Personal Loans

Personal Loan EMI — Everything You Need to Know

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that doesn't require any collateral. Banks and NBFCs offer personal loans based on your credit score, income and repayment capacity. The EMI depends on the loan amount, interest rate and tenure you choose.

💰 Loan Amount

Personal loans in India range from ₹50,000 to ₹50 lakhs depending on your income and credit score. Most salaried individuals qualify for 10-20x their monthly salary.

📅 Loan Tenure

Personal loans have a tenure of 1 to 7 years. Shorter tenure means higher EMI but less total interest. Longer tenure means lower EMI but more total interest paid.

⚡ Quick Disbursement

Personal loans from NBFCs like Navi, MoneyView and Finnable are disbursed within 24-48 hours of approval, making them ideal for emergencies.

📊 No Collateral

Unlike home or car loans, personal loans don't require any asset as security. Your creditworthiness is the only criteria for approval.

FAQ

Personal Loan EMI — Common Questions

What is the EMI for a ₹5 lakh personal loan?+
For a ₹5 lakh personal loan at 13% interest for 3 years, the EMI is approximately ₹16,820 per month. Use our calculator above to calculate for different amounts and tenures.
What is the minimum salary for a personal loan?+
Most banks require a minimum monthly salary of ₹15,000-25,000 for personal loans. NBFCs like Navi and MoneyView have lower income requirements starting from ₹12,000/month.
What CIBIL score is needed for personal loan?+
A CIBIL score of 700 or above is generally required. A score above 750 gets you the best interest rates. Scores below 650 may lead to rejection or very high rates.
Can I get a personal loan without salary slip?+
Yes, some NBFCs like MoneyView and Navi offer personal loans based on bank statements instead of salary slips. Self-employed individuals can use ITR as income proof.
